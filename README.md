- **The Vampire:** Upcoming Content. Stay tuned...
- **Constitution:** The so-called "Heaven-Descendant Blade". It's origins are unknown, but what is known is that carries the essence of a long forgotten galaxy inside it. It radiates an aura of stellar fog everywhere it goes, as seen in it's fight background. It's motives remain a mystery as well, at least, for now. Perhaps if you prove yourself worthy, you'll be able to wield it yourself...
- **Urceris, The Forgotten Halberd:** Upcoming Content. Stay tuned...
- **Nightmare Rose:** A nasty conglomerate of corrupt enemies. Why it chooses to disguise as a massive rose is up to the wildest imagination's explanation. It is known to mysteriously pop up in farmer's fields and public gardens alike, corrupting the surrounding area, earning it the name "The Botanist's curse". Perhaps if you can peel away its petals, you might come to know it's origin...
- **The Wyvern Corpse:** The remains of The Great Wyvern. Among the most powerful and prestigeous wyverns to soar the higher orbits of Terraria. What or who brought it down remains a mystery. It lives a simple life under the Crimson Hivemind, scavenging for any flesh it can land its bony claws on, including the flesh of other members of the hivemind. Its soul remains trapped, yearning to return to the sky where it belongs. Perhaps if you can batter up its decaying body enough, you can allow The Great Wyvern to make its final ascension.

- **Haepien, the Nature Sprite:** Upcoming Content. Stay tuned...

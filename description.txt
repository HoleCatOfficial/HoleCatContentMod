[h1] TALID [/h1]

The Talid Mod is a content mod that adds a multitude of new items, a damage class, 3 bosses, and 3 minibosses.
Content exists to see all throughout progression, with WorldGen Structures, a Town NPC, new ores, and more!

(I'm not very good at advertising)

[h3] The Ideal Mod Experience [/h3]

This segment is much less a mandate on how to play the mod as it is recommendations that I’d make if you want to have the best experience with Talid.


[b] Recommended [/b]

[list]
[*] Fargo’s Souls: Talid has official compatibility with Eternity and Masochist mode. Eternity and Masochist mode also offer the fullest exploration of Talid’s story. 

The Eternity Mode bossfights can be experienced in Calamity Mod’s Death Mode, but normal enemy reworks and eternity drops will not be included.

[*] Remnants: Talid has support for Remnants both in worldgen and in QoL parity. Support will only get stronger with time.

[*] Calamity: Fables: Talid only adds titlecard support at the moment, but expect to see further cross-mod integration with Fables in the future.

[*] Vanilla Exposure: It is recommended that you play Vanilla Terraria through at least once before approaching Talid. There is content that makes the most sense if you have prior exposure to Vanilla Terraria.


[/list]

[b] Not Recommended: [/b]

[list]
[*] Auto Potion Mods: Any mod that auto-consumes healing potions is not recommended to be played with Talid. Talid has its own mechanic of this nature and the two would interfere. That said, Talid does have a system to deal with Fargo’s implementation of this mechanic, so the two can still be played together.

[*] Zenith Modification Mods: Talid’s ideal experience puts the Zenith at just after moonlord. Talid alters mod recipes to keep the Zenith at this progression point. Not post-Yharon, not post-Son, post-moonlord. Mods that alter the zenith to accommodate other mods recipe changes, such as adding post-moonlord calamity profiles to the zenith, are not recommended for this reason.
[/list]

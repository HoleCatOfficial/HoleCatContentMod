# TALID MOD

The Talid mod is a medium-sized content mod that adds the following:

### 3 Bosses
- Constitution: Fought before the Wall of Flesh using a summon bought from the merchant. It is the source of the Stellar Item set.

- Nightmare Rose: A stationary conglomeration that stands to be the last stand of the corruption after the Moon Lord is defeated. Summoned using a book titled "The Botanist's Curse".
- Wyvern Corpse: The rotten body of the Great Wyvern, a once immensely powerful creature of the sky. Summoned using the carcass of a vicious bunny.

### 2 Minibosses:
- Ichor Node: An amplifier of the Crimson's power, and a defender against invaders such as you.
- Cursed Flame Node: The corrupt counterpart to the Ichor Node.

### A Hardmode Biome
- The Rift is an artifical biome obtainable after a solar eclipse happens. It induces suffocation unless you enter with proper gear.

### A custom damage class
- Scepters are a halfway between Thrower and Ranged. The primary fire varies, but every scepter can be thrown using right click, functioning like a boomerang. The class has armor and accessories, but cannot recieve reforges.
